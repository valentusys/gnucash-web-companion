## Publication decision

Decision: prepare `v0.2.1-writealpha` as an unpublished candidate and stop before publication.

Rationale:

- A maintenance write-alpha candidate is warranted because Phases 173–180 add meaningful copied/disposable dogfood evidence plus UX/API hardening after published `v0.2.0-writealpha`.
- The gate is locally green and safety defaults remain intact.
- This phase is not explicit owner authorization to publish. Therefore no tag or GitHub release is created.

- Publication is blocked until explicit Val authorization and a fresh pre-publish gate.
- Write-alpha remains experimental, disabled by default, and executable only with `APP_ENV=test` when explicitly enabled.
- Real/private-book write safety is not established and not claimed.
- Compatibility evidence remains intentionally narrow; no broad GnuCash version/backend compatibility claim is made.
- CSV export remains synchronous and capped at 10,000 rows.
- The project remains pre-alpha, not production-ready, and not security-audited.
